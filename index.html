CrunchCall

Put out a CrunchCall.

See which colleagues are heading to lunch, and join them in one tap.

Friends only

Only people you've added can see you, and you choose exactly who.

No tracking

No GPS and no ads. Your place is just a name you pick, like "Mess" or "Hub".

Gone when you're done

Lunch statuses end by themselves after 30 to 90 minutes.
