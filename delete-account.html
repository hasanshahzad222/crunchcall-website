Delete your account

You can delete your CrunchCall account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

Open CrunchCall, go to Profile, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

Can't use the app? Email hello@crunchcall.app from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my CrunchCall account". We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Your account and the data linked to it, such as your profile (name, username, job, photo), friends, lunch statuses and settings.
