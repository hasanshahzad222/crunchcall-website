Child safety standards

Our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Zero tolerance

CrunchCall does not allow any content or behaviour that sexually exploits or endangers children. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexualising minors, or any attempt to contact a child for sexual purposes. Breaking this rule leads to immediate removal of the content and permanent removal of the account.

Adults only

CrunchCall is for adult staff at workplaces such as hospitals, to coordinate lunch with colleagues. You must be 18 or over to use it.

Reporting in the app

Anyone can report a person or a photo from inside the app, and block anyone at any time. Reported content is hidden for the person who reported it straight away, and we review every report.

What we do

When we find or are told about CSAE or CSAM, we remove it, ban the account, keep what is needed as evidence, and report it to the appropriate authorities, including the police, the UK's National Crime Agency (CEOP) and the Internet Watch Foundation, and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where relevant. We cooperate fully with law enforcement.

Contact

To report a child safety concern, or for any questions about these standards, email hello@crunchcall.app. If a child is in immediate danger, call 999 (UK) or your local emergency number.
